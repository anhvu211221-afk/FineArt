Everyday Happiness


2025 | Oil paint, Papier Mache | 35 × 31 in

I document everyday emotional shifts by molding masks directly from my face over nine consecutive days, symbolizing a full cycle from beginning to end. Through daily repetition, the process captures subtle inner changes over time. Inspired by Vietnamese theatre face painting (Tuồng), I use flat colors - red, pink, blue, green, yellow, orange, and white - to express emotional states while connecting traditional visual language with contemporary experience. Working with papier-mâché becomes a mindful ritual connecting my breath, hands, face, and materials to the present moment. The masks intentionally lack eye holes, inviting viewers to complete the work through imagination and personal interpretation. Through this process, I explore how emotional meaning is shaped through self, material, ritual, and audience interaction.

Cocooned


2025 | Oil paint | 15 x 11 in

The work explores the fragile space between protection and restraint as an inner self begins to form. The cocoon-like shell acts as both shelter and limitation, protecting her while slowing her growth. The deep red background becomes the heat and pressure of transformation, while soft drifting light suggests uncertain memories and unseen futures. The piece captures a moment of becoming: the self slowly stretching and breaking open toward a new identity.

Light Installation


2025 | Acrylic panels | 100 × 300 in

This installation transforms a tunnel hallway into a reflection on the relationship between nature and constructed space. Using suspended plastic panels, light, color, and reflection within three cage-like structures, the work recreates the rhythms of nature through synthetic materials. Inspired by the movement of light through leaves, water, glass, and air, I explore how artificial materials can imitate natural atmosphere and motion. The installation becomes a way of “painting” a tree through light and structure, suggesting roots, trunk, and canopy through layered forms and shifting illumination. Through transparency and improvisation, the work questions how fragments of nature can be reimagined within industrial and human-made environments.

Memories


2022 | Mixed media

A wearable sculpture inspired by the Eight of Swords tarot card, this work depicts a feeling of uncertainty and emotional confinement. The figure is blinded, surrounded by a flow of memories, symbolizing the act of retreating into the past to avoid pain and refusing to face the truth. The wearable form reflects the tension between protection and self-imposed limitation.
